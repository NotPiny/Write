Write is a simple yet powerful library for adding custom content such as blocks and items to the game whilst mimicking vanilla functionality as much as possible. _Using Write is as simple as installing it. Seriously, we will handle **EVERYTHING** from there on out with sensible defaults._

### Network Disclosure
By default, Write will automatically send off resource packs to [mcpacks.dev](https://mcpacks.dev/). This service has no sort of affiliation with the plugin. While I have personally vetted both the [terms of service](https://mcpacks.dev/terms) and [privacy policy](https://mcpacks.dev/privacy) as of Aug 9th 2026, I cannot make any guarantee this remains by the time you download the plugin. If you do not wish to send them your files you may disable the cloud resource pack functionality in the `config.yml`

Additionally, we use [FastStats](https://faststats.dev) for analytics. You may wish to review their privacy policy, or opt out by editing `plugins/faststats/config.properties`.

While I could try to document everything here I'd instead advise you to check out the [official documentation](https://docs.piny.dev/write/).
